SAFEGUARDING
AT THE RHINOS.

Fareham Rhinos is committed to creating a safe environment for children, young people and vulnerable adults taking part in our club.

Your safeguarding lead

ASHLEY
JOHNSON

Ashley Johnson is the club's Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) and is responsible for safeguarding and child protection issues at Fareham Rhinos.

Any safeguarding concern, no matter how small, should be reported promptly. You can speak to Ashley directly at the club or use the confidential reporting form below.

Report a concern
01 Welfare first

The welfare of the child or vulnerable adult is the paramount concern.

02 Report concerns

Don't investigate a concern yourself. Record the facts and report it.

03 Handled discreetly

Safeguarding concerns are handled with discretion and confidentiality wherever possible.

IS SOMEONE IN IMMEDIATE DANGER?

Do not wait for a response from this website. Call 999. For urgent safeguarding concerns you can also contact Hampshire Children's Services on 0300 555 1384.

Club policy

OUR SAFEGUARDING
COMMITMENT.

Our safeguarding policy sets out the club's approach to protecting children and vulnerable adults, the responsibilities of coaches, volunteers, parents and members, and the procedure for responding to concerns.

The policy includes safe recruitment, safeguarding training, a code of conduct, incident reporting and referral to appropriate safeguarding agencies where required.
